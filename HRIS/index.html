05The portal app

One shared device, built to resist abuse.

The portal runs on one shared device per outlet. Staff find their name, confirm their shift and clock in with a face scan. It's the most safeguarded surface, because attendance ties directly to payroll.

Face matching

Verifies the person clocking in is who they claim to be, closing the door on buddy punching.

Location range

Clock in only works within 100m of the outlet, with a GPS buffer for outlets inside malls.

No clock tricks

Attendance runs on server time, so changing the device clock does nothing, and tampering is detected and blocked.

06The operational user app

Built around the two things staff do most.

Operational staff use their own phone to request overtime and leave, check payslips and keep their profile current. Every step we didn't have time to build was cut.

99.99%of 3,941 new joiners (Feb 2023 to Nov 2025) already onboarded with a Gmail account.

With no time to build account provisioning, Continue with Google removed an entire onboarding step and matched how staff already signed in. One tap, no passwords to manage.

Requests, front and center

Overtime and leave sit at the top of the home screen as prominent cards with a direct New Request action.

Self service profile

Staff view and edit their own personal, employment and payroll details, cutting back and forth with HR.

A limited selection of screens. The full app is under NDA.

07The trial

5 outlets, 5 days, and one big surprise.

Before rolling out to 165 outlets, we trialed live attendance from 6 to 10 March. Three outlets ran with geo fencing on, and two warehouses ran without it, since warehouse staff are highly mobile.

What went wrong
76.7%of valid check ins were out of range: 174 of 227, on the first two days (6 to 7 March).
  1. Operations had defined both the range and the stored coordinates for every outlet.
  2. Some ranges were as low as 1.9 to 2.5m, below what phone GPS can achieve.
  3. Several outlet coordinates were themselves inaccurate.
  4. With natural drift of 15 to 30m in malls and shophouses, staff standing at the counter were rejected.
Scale risk. With 165 outlets going live at the end of March, the same misconfiguration would have multiplied platform wide.
Why

GPS accuracy isn't fixed.

The error depends heavily on the environment, so one tiny threshold could never work everywhere.

Open sky, street facing

Street kiosks, open car parks, outdoor counters.

Typical GPS error
3 to 10m
Recommended threshold
15 to 25m

Mall, urban block

Ground floor retail, shophouses, dense city blocks.

Typical GPS error
15 to 30m
Recommended threshold
25 to 50m

Indoor, basement, upper floor

Mall basements, upper floor units, enclosed offices.

Typical GPS error
30 to 100m
Recommended threshold
50 to 100m
The fix

Two root causes, two fixes.

FIX 1 · DATA

Correct the coordinates

Each outlet shared its live location, and we updated the invalid latitude and longitude so the geo fence centred on the real spot.

FIX 2 · RANGE50m

One universal range

GPS buffer included, applied as a single admin config change. No development, no manager workload, still requiring staff to be at the outlet.

Validated in the trial
100%successful check ins once coordinates were corrected, with no more location complaints.

With the 50m range, every clock in fell inside the boundary. Of 22 records on 9 March (15:40 to 23:19), the mean distance was 7.20m, the minimum 1.22m and the maximum 15.19m.
