02Objectives

Help in the moment, fewer complaints.

  • Assist users having QR scanning trouble in real time
  • Reduce user complaints through better in app scanning support
03Research

We tested what actually breaks a scan.

Controlled experiments on printer quality, QR code density and camera capability, across iOS and Android.

  1. Flattening receipts dramatically improved scan success
  2. Print area size, small or large, had no impact on success
  3. iOS did better than Android on bent or damaged receipts
  4. The Hangry app camera beat the native cameras on both platforms
04Competitive benchmark

Most apps offered a lonely “?”.

Most competitors had no scanning instructions, just a "?" icon with no visual guidance. Only Dana and Grab explained the QR code and had a help section with illustrations, a clear direction for our solution.

05Solution

Help only when you ask for it.

Iteration 01 · Rejected

Mandatory instruction page before scanning

Blocked every user before they could scan, adding friction to the happy path. Dropped because users who didn't need help might abandon.

Iteration 02 · Shipped

Contextual bottom sheet, scenario specific

A help sheet opened during scanning for specific cases: folded receipts, water damage, poor lighting. User initiated, never forced, with a clear CTA to scan again.
