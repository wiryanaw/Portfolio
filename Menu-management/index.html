Wiryana Streamlined Menu Management
POS Internal Tool 2022

Scaling multi platform operations with 97.8% efficiency improvement

I led the design of the menu management project, one of the key initiatives aimed at helping the company reduce costs. By unifying menu control across 8 brands and 3 platforms into a single interface, the tool increased operational efficiency by 97.78% and saved IDR 64 million in hardware costs.

Context

The terms
Materials

Physical items or ingredients required to prepare or serve menu items. Materials are essential components for both parent and child menus, and their availability impacts the ability to offer related menu items.

Parent menu

The main menu item or dish that is offered on the menu. It is the primary option that may have various variants or sizes (child menus) associated with it.

Child menu

A variant or specific option related to a parent menu item, such as different sizes, flavors, or customization choices. Child menus depend on the parent menu and may be affected by the availability of materials.

Design Explorations

The Design Constraints
POS screen size. Limited resolution of 1024 × 768 required a compact, scannable layout
Poor display quality — Colors needed to be high contrast and clearly visible under poor screen conditions
Familiar terminology. Used the language outlet staff already knew: Parent menu = Menu, Child menu = Kompleks menu
